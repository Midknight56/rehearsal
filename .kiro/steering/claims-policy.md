# Claims policy
- Never fabricate evidence, test output, measurements or user feedback. If a check did not run, say so.
- Attribute work to the tool that did it. Do not describe the work of another tool as Kiro's, or the reverse.
- State model limits next to results. Graph shape, simulation output and reports are not safety, correctness or legal claims.
- Performance, privacy and accessibility statements require a recorded check on named hardware or a named tool; otherwise they are targets.
- Do not weaken a test to make it pass. Preserve the reproducer and report it.
- Append every completed task to docs/kiro-log.md the same day.
