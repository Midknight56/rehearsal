**Units and clock**
- The engine counts time as integer milliseconds. Seconds are converted once, where input is checked, and never inside the engine.
- The engine never reads the wall clock and never calls `Math.random`. Every random draw comes from a stream keyed by the seed, replication, attendee and station.
- The same scenario, seed and replication therefore give the same result, byte for byte.
- The "30 paired runs took 0.8 s on this device" line is the page's own timing display, not engine time.
These are scenario results under the app's assumptions, not a forecast. The Harbor Hall Community Fair and its 120 attendees are fictional and illustrative. I'm not recommending a staffing level.
